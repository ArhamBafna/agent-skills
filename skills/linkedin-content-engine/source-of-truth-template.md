# FICTIONAL EXAMPLE (for reference — delete before you run)

## 1. One-line identity
**Meridian is the revenue-visibility layer for B2B finance teams.** We turn messy CRM and billing data into one forecast the whole company trusts.
- Founder / content voice: Dana Reyes, CEO
- Positioning: "Your forecast is only as good as the data underneath it." "Stop closing the books on a guess."

## 2. What we do
Meridian connects your CRM, billing, and ERP into one live revenue model, so finance stops rebuilding the forecast in a spreadsheet every month.

## 3. The problem we solve
**Core problem:** Finance teams forecast on data they don't trust, so they hedge, and leadership plans on a number that's already wrong.
**The villain:** The month-end spreadsheet scramble.
**Failure modes:** Three systems give three numbers. The forecast is stale by the time it ships. Nobody can trace a number back to its source.

## 4. Beliefs
1. A forecast nobody trusts is worse than no forecast, because people plan on it anyway.
2. More dashboards don't build trust. One reconciled number does.
3. Finance shouldn't be a data-cleaning team. That's a tooling failure, not a headcount problem.

## 5. What we are NOT
- Not a BI tool you have to build yourself.
- Not another dashboard on top of the same untrusted data.

## 6. Proof points
- Regional SaaS company: forecast variance cut from 22% to 6% in one quarter.
- Series B fintech: month-end close from 9 days to 3.

## 7. ICP
- B2B SaaS, $10M–$100M ARR, has a real finance team feeling forecast pain.
- Disqualifier: pre-revenue, or a one-spreadsheet startup that isn't hurting yet.

## 8. Personas + JTBD
| Persona | Who | Primary JTBD |
|---|---|---|
| CFO | Owns the number to the board | Ship a forecast I'd stake my credibility on |
| Head of RevOps | Owns the data plumbing | Stop reconciling three systems by hand |
| FP&A Lead | Builds the model | Trace every number to its source in seconds |

## 9. Voice & messaging cues
- Tone: plain, operator-to-operator, no finance jargon for its own sake.
- Signature vocabulary: reconciled, source of truth, forecast variance, trust the number.
- Recurring contrasts: trusted vs. hedged, one number vs. three, source of truth vs. spreadsheet.
