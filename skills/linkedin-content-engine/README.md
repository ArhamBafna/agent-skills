# Weekly LinkedIn Content Engine

Turn the things you already do into a week of on-voice LinkedIn posts, in Claude Code. Pull fresh signal from your own posts and a couple of creators you learn from, filter every idea through your strategy and beliefs, and draft posts in your voice, on a weekly loop.

> Your build-along project for the Maven live session "Build a LinkedIn Content Agent That Writes in Your Voice."
> Built by Catalyst, the preferred GTM content agency, led by Will Leatherman. gotcatalyst.com

## The one idea

AI is bad at having a point of view and great at research and formatting. So the leverage isn't the prompt. It's the system around the writer: a source of truth that holds your positioning, ICP, and beliefs, and a filter that only lets through ideas worth one of your posting slots.

**Idea first, formatting second.** You can't fix a bad idea with a good hook. Every move here protects the idea.

## What you leave with

- A `source-of-truth.md` that holds your positioning, ICP, buyer personas, and beliefs
- A `SKILL.md` that pulls signal, filters ideas against your strategy, and drafts posts in your voice
- A weekly loop that hands you a ranked idea bank plus 5 ready-to-review drafts

## Quickstart (follow along live)

1. Install Claude Code and open this folder. See [PREREQUISITES.md](PREREQUISITES.md).
2. Fill in `source-of-truth-template.md` and save it as `source-of-truth.md`.
3. Read `SKILL.md`, connect your sources, and run it once.

## The three moves, in order

1. Define your source of truth (positioning, ICP, personas, beliefs) so the filter has something to judge against.
2. Pull signal from your own posts and a couple of creators, then rank ideas by fit with your ICP, not by vanity engagement.
3. Draft in your voice and put it on a loop, with a human review step before anything publishes.
