## 4. Beat Sheet & Timing Gates

| Gate | Timestamp | Narrative Goal | Visual Focus | Quality Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook** | `0.0s - 2.0s` | Grab immediate attention | High-impact typography entrance or kinetic logo lock | Must feel punchy in first 1.5s |
| **Problem** | `2.0s - 5.0s` | Frame the friction or chaos | Fast-moving glitch or fragmented cards | High contrast, clear hierarchy |
| **Solution** | `5.0s - 10.0s` | Product smoothly solves it | UI morphs into organized dashboard, metrics tick up | Closed-form spring with subtle overshoot |
| **Climax** | `10.0s - 13.0s` | Core differentiator / speed | Rapid kinetic camera zoom or multi-device layout | Responsive, no clipped text |
| **Outro** | `13.0s - 15.0s` | Call to action & settle | Final brand emblem, URL, action button | Clean settle into resting state |

---

## 5. Visual Critique Criteria (Scorecard)
During the critique pass, evaluate keyframe stills against this 5-point standard (each rated 1-10; target average >= 8/10):

1. **Composition & Bounding**: No clipped text, no overlapping labels, elements comfortably padded from canvas edges.
2. **Contrast & Legibility**: Type is instantly readable against backgrounds; WCAG AA compliant contrast.
3. **Motion Physics**: Elements accelerate and decelerate with spring mass; zero robotic linear tweens.
4. **Pacing & Beat Alignment**: Transitions occur on key musical beats or steady 2-3 second rhythm.
5. **Brand Polish**: Looks custom-designed; zero generic AI-slop hallmarks (no random purple-blue gradient soup, no unstyled boxes).
